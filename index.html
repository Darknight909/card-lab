Standards used

This build uses publicly published standards as grading caps/guidelines. Professional graders may consider additional factors and subjective eye appeal. Standards can change; verify before submission.

PSA

PSA 10 requires a virtually perfect card; published centering tolerance is approximately 55/45 or better on the front and 75/25 on the back. Corners, focus, gloss, stains and printing flaws also matter.

Official PSA standards

Beckett / BGS

BGS evaluates centering, corners, edges and surface. Its published scale gives Pristine 10 centering as 50/50 on the front and 55/45 or better on the back; Gem Mint 9.5 permits 50/50 one direction and 55/45 the other on the front. Beckett states that the overall grade is not a simple average and the lowest subgrade is heavily weighted.

Official Beckett scale

CGC Cards

CGC Cards evaluates centering, corners, edges and surface on its 10-point scale. Pristine 10 is reserved for exceptionally strong cards; phone-photo estimates remain conservative because microscopic defects cannot be ruled out.

Official CGC scale

SGC

SGC uses 1–10 with half grades plus Pristine 10 and Gem 10. Its published descriptions evaluate centering, focus, corners, surface gloss, print/refractor lines, stains and wear.

Official SGC scale

Cloudflare backend

The Worker coordinates Google Vision OCR/visual discovery, trusted-source identity verification, visible-condition analysis, and current eBay listings when the eBay API is connected. Your collection remains on this phone.

The Worker URL and API key are stored only in this browser's local storage. Do not put the API key in GitHub.

Backup & privacy

Your collection and compressed card photos are stored locally in this browser's IndexedDB. They are not stored in cloud storage by Card Lab. During automatic analysis, analysis copies of the front/back photos are sent transiently through your Cloudflare Worker to Google Cloud Vision for OCR/visual-web matching and to Cloudflare Workers AI for visible-condition inspection. Tavily receives text/search clues only. If official eBay image search is enabled, an analysis copy of the front is also sent transiently to eBay. The Worker does not write photos or collection data to KV, R2, D1, or Durable Objects.

Full recovery includes the private backend API key and current draft photos. Store that file securely.
