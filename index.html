Standards used

This build uses publicly published standards as grading caps/guidelines. Professional graders may consider additional factors and subjective eye appeal. Standards can change; verify before submission.

PSA

PSA 10 requires a virtually perfect card; published centering tolerance is approximately 55/45 or better on the front and 75/25 on the back. Corners, focus, gloss, stains and printing flaws also matter.

Official PSA standards

Beckett / BGS

BGS evaluates centering, corners, edges and surface. Published 10 centering is 50/50 on front; 9.5 allows approximately 50/50 one direction and 55/45 the other. Overall grade is not a simple average; the lowest subgrade heavily constrains the result.

Official Beckett scale

CGC Cards

CGC Pristine 10 calls for 50/50 centering and virtually flawless condition. Gem Mint 10 allows approximately 55/45 front and 75/25 reverse, with high corner/edge/surface requirements.

Official CGC scale

SGC

SGC uses 1–10 with half grades plus Pristine 10 and Gem 10. Its published descriptions evaluate centering, focus, corners, surface gloss, print/refractor lines, stains and wear.

Official SGC scale

Cloudflare backend

The Worker performs automatic identification and visible-condition analysis. Your collection remains on this phone.

The Worker URL and API key are stored only in this browser's local storage. Do not put the API key in GitHub.

Backup & privacy

Your collection and compressed card photos are stored locally in this browser's IndexedDB. They are not stored in cloud storage by this app. During automatic analysis, the selected front/back photos are sent transiently to your Cloudflare Worker/Workers AI and are not written to KV, R2, D1, or Durable Objects.

Install on iPhone

Open the hosted app in DuckDuckGo. If "Add to Home Screen" is available, use it. If not, open the same URL once in Safari, Share → Add to Home Screen → Open as Web App. After installation it launches from its own icon.
